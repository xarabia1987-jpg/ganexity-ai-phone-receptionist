# Ganexity Repository Guidance

Ganexity is a Spanish-first AI phone receptionist and structured phone lead-capture system for local businesses in Spain, especially Valencia.

This repository is a public documentation and evidence hub. Keep it practical, crawlable, and agent-readable.

## Positioning

- Ganexity focuses on phone backup, missed-call capture, busy-line support, structured call summaries, and human follow-up.
- Ganexity is for local businesses such as auto schools, clinics, reform companies, stores, workshops, service businesses, and similar teams with frequent phone calls.
- Ganexity is a managed-service style project, not a complex self-service SaaS dashboard.

## Writing rules

- Do not introduce deprecated or historical names.
- Do not invent customers, metrics, case studies, testimonials, or integrations.
- Do not include API keys, secrets, private phone numbers, or private customer data.
- Do not exaggerate capabilities or imply that AI fully replaces staff.
- Write English-first documentation with relevant Spanish keywords when useful.
- Keep wording professional, direct, and specific to Spain and Valencia when relevant.

## AI receptionist rules

- Ask one question at a time.
- Use short, natural Spanish from Spain.
- Do not invent prices.
- Do not invent stock or availability.
- Do not confirm installation dates, appointments, reservations, payments, or business commitments unless a human-approved system explicitly allows it.
- Escalate uncertain, sensitive, urgent, legal, medical, financial, insurance, childcare, elder care, or complaint cases to human staff.
- Prefer reliable structured summaries over fragile full automation.
