# AI Phone Receptionist for Marketing Agencies Phone Intake

Business type: Marketing Agencies Phone Intake
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Marketing agencies receive new lead calls that need clear project qualification.
Spanish keywords: agencia marketing, intake telefónico, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Creative and business service teams are often in production, meetings, or client work. Ganexity can capture project scope, deadlines, contact details, and callback requests.

## Common incoming calls

- quote requests
- project scope questions
- deadline questions
- file or specification questions
- meeting requests
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final quote
- creative scope and deliverables
- delivery date commitment
- contract terms
- usage rights
- payments and complaints

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
