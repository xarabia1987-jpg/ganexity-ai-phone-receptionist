# AI Phone Receptionist for Wedding Venues

Business type: Wedding Venues
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Wedding venues receive high-value enquiries with many details and preferred dates.
Spanish keywords: finca bodas, salón bodas, captación de llamadas

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Hospitality teams receive calls during service, check-in, events, and peak demand. Ganexity can capture reservation or enquiry details and pass them to staff for confirmation.

## Common incoming calls

- reservation request intake
- availability questions
- group or event enquiries
- menu, room, or service questions
- changes and cancellations
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final reservation or booking confirmation
- room or table availability
- event contracts
- prices and deposits
- cancellations and refunds
- special-case commitments

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
