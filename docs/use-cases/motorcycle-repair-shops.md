# AI Phone Receptionist for Motorcycle Repair Shops

Business type: Motorcycle Repair Shops
Region focus: Spain and Valencia
Primary phone problem: Motorcycle workshops receive calls about repairs, parts, inspections, and urgent issues.
Spanish keywords: taller motos, reparación motos, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Automotive businesses often work in noisy spaces, with staff focused on vehicles, parts, and customers on site. Ganexity can capture vehicle details, service needs, urgency, and callback preferences when the line is busy.

## Common incoming calls

- repair estimates and diagnostics
- appointment request intake
- vehicle drop-off questions
- parts or tire availability questions
- service status updates
- urgent breakdown or callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final repair price
- parts or tire stock confirmation
- vehicle safety decisions
- appointment confirmation
- warranty decisions
- payments and complaints

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
