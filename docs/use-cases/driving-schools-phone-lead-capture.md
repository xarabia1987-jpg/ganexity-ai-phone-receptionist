# AI Phone Receptionist for Driving School Phone Lead Capture

Business type: Driving School Phone Lead Capture
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Missed student enquiries can become lost leads if nobody follows up quickly.
Spanish keywords: captación de leads telefónicos, autoescuela, backup telefónico para negocios

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Driving schools receive frequent calls while instructors are in practical lessons and reception teams are helping students. Ganexity can capture enquiries about courses, documents, schedules, and callback requests for autoescuelas in Spain and Valencia.

## Common incoming calls

- course prices and packages
- license categories and requirements
- theory class questions
- practical lesson availability
- documents needed for enrollment
- rescheduling and callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final prices and discounts
- confirmed lesson slots
- exam dates or results
- payments and refunds
- instructor assignment
- business commitments

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
