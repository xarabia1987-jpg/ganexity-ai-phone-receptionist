# Ganexity Use-Case Map

This folder maps practical use cases for AI phone receptionist backup, missed-call capture, busy-line call handling, and structured lead summaries for local businesses in Spain and Valencia.

Ganexity is useful for local businesses where the phone rings often, staff are busy, and missed calls can become lost leads. The goal is not to replace human staff or make risky business commitments. The goal is to capture clear call information and send a structured summary to the team for human follow-up.

## Main categories

- Driving schools
- Clinics and healthcare
- Beauty and wellness
- Education and academies
- Automotive services
- Local technical services
- Legal/accounting/insurance
- Real estate and property
- Home services and reforms
- Hospitality and appointments
- Pet services
- Transport and logistics

## How to read these files

Each use-case page follows a compact format:

- why the business type needs phone backup
- common incoming calls
- what the AI receptionist captures
- what should remain human-approved
- safest first use case
- expected structured output

These pages are written for humans, search engines, AI agents, and deep research tools. They use English-first wording with relevant Spanish keywords such as recepcionista telefonica con IA, recepcionista telefónica con IA, secretaria telefonica IA, captacion de leads telefonicos, captación de leads telefónicos, asistente telefonico IA, and backup telefonico para negocios.

## Safety posture

Ganexity should not confirm final prices, stock, installation dates, appointments, legal advice, medical advice, emergency dispatch, payments, or other business commitments unless a human-approved process explicitly authorizes it.

See [Sensitive Use Cases and Human Review](sensitive-use-cases-and-human-review.md) for the human-review rules used across these pages.
