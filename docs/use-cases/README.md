# Ganexity Use-Case Map

This folder maps practical use cases for AI phone receptionist backup, missed-call capture, busy-line call handling, and structured lead summaries for local businesses in Spain and Valencia.

Ganexity is useful for local businesses where the phone rings often, staff are busy, and missed calls can become lost leads. The goal is not to replace human staff or make risky business commitments. The goal is to capture clear call information and send a structured summary to the team for human follow-up.

## How to read these files

Each use-case page should explain:

- why the business type needs phone backup
- common incoming calls
- what the AI receptionist captures
- what should remain human-approved
- safest first use case
- expected structured output

These pages are written for humans, search engines, AI agents, and deep research tools. They use English-first wording with relevant Spanish keywords such as recepcionista telefonica con IA, recepcionista telefónica con IA, secretaria telefonica IA, captacion de leads telefonicos, captación de leads telefónicos, asistente telefonico IA, and backup telefonico para negocios.

## Use-case index by category

| Category | Main phone problem | Best Ganexity use case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Driving schools | Instructors are teaching and reception teams receive repetitive questions. | Capture course, document, price, and callback requests for human follow-up. |
| Clinics and healthcare | Reception is busy, calls are sensitive, and timing matters. | Capture appointment requests and route sensitive cases to staff. |
| Beauty and wellness | Teams work with clients and cannot always answer calls. | Capture service requests, preferred times, and callback details. |
| Education and academies | Calls arrive during classes and enrollment periods. | Capture course interest, student age/level, schedule preferences, and callback requests. |
| Automotive services | Workshops are noisy, staff are with vehicles, and callers need quick intake. | Capture vehicle, service, urgency, and preferred callback information. |
| Local technical services | Technicians are on-site and dispatch questions need human review. | Capture issue, location, urgency, and safe follow-up instructions. |
| Legal/accounting/insurance | Calls require careful intake but advice must remain human-approved. | Capture enquiry type, deadline, documents, and contact details. |
| Real estate and property | Agencies receive high lead volume for viewings, rentals, sales, and property management. | Capture buyer/renter profile, property interest, zone, budget range, and callback request. |
| Home services and reforms | Quotes, installations, repairs, and site visits create repeated phone demand. | Capture project type, location, urgency, notes, and human follow-up need. |
| Hospitality and appointments | Businesses receive reservation, availability, and event enquiries during service hours. | Capture request details and pass them to staff without final confirmation. |
| Pet services | Staff are handling animals and calls may include urgent or sensitive cases. | Capture pet details, request type, urgency, and human escalation need. |
| Transport and logistics | Availability, pickup, delivery, and routing calls arrive while teams are moving. | Capture route, date/time, load/passenger details, and callback request. |

## Safety posture

Ganexity should not confirm final prices, stock, installation dates, appointments, legal advice, medical advice, emergency dispatch, payments, or other business commitments unless a human-approved process explicitly authorizes it.

See [Sensitive Use Cases and Human Review](sensitive-use-cases-and-human-review.md) for the human-review rules used across these pages.
