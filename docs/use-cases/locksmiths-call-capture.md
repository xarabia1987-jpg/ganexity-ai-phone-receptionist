# AI Phone Receptionist for Locksmiths

Business type: Locksmiths
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Locksmith calls may be urgent and need fast human review.
Spanish keywords: cerrajero, captación de llamadas, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Technical service teams are often on the road or on-site, while urgent calls keep arriving. Ganexity can capture the issue, location, urgency, and callback details without promising dispatch or arrival times.

## Common incoming calls

- urgent repair requests
- quote requests
- service area questions
- equipment or issue details
- availability questions
- after-hours callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final quote
- dispatch confirmation
- arrival time promise
- safety instructions
- parts availability
- payments and complaints

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For urgent local service calls, Ganexity should not promise dispatch, arrival times, or emergency outcomes. It should capture the problem and alert the team.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
