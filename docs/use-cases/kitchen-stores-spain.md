# AI Phone Receptionist for Kitchen Stores in Spain

Business type: Kitchen Stores in Spain
Region focus: Spain and Valencia
Primary phone problem: Kitchen stores receive calls while staff are helping showroom visitors.
Spanish keywords: tienda de cocinas, cocinas España, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Showroom and retail teams are often serving walk-in customers when phone leads arrive. Ganexity can capture product interest, service needs, city or zone, and callback preferences.

## Common incoming calls

- product questions
- showroom visit requests
- stock or delivery questions
- measurement or installation questions
- quote requests
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final prices
- stock availability
- delivery dates
- installation dates
- discounts
- design or measurement commitments

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
