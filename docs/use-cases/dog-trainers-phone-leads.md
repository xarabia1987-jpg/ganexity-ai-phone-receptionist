# AI Phone Receptionist for Dog Trainers

Business type: Dog Trainers
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Dog trainers receive calls about behavior issues, classes, and private sessions.
Spanish keywords: adiestrador canino, educación canina, captación de leads telefónicos

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Pet service teams are often handling animals and may receive urgent owner concerns. Ganexity can capture pet details, service needs, urgency, and human follow-up requirements.

## Common incoming calls

- appointment request intake
- service availability questions
- pet details and needs
- urgent concerns requiring escalation
- rescheduling
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- veterinary advice or triage
- emergency response
- final appointment confirmation
- pricing
- safety decisions
- complaints

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For veterinary and pet care use cases, Ganexity should not provide veterinary advice, emergency triage, diagnosis, or treatment guidance. It should capture the request and escalate.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
