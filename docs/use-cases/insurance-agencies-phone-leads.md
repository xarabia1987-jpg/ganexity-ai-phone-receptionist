# AI Phone Receptionist for Insurance Agencies

Business type: Insurance Agencies
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Insurance agencies receive calls about policies, claims, renewals, and quotes.
Spanish keywords: agencia de seguros, leads telefónicos, asistente telefónico IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Professional offices receive document-heavy and deadline-sensitive calls while staff are in meetings or focused work. Ganexity can capture the enquiry and route it for human review without giving advice.

## Common incoming calls

- new enquiry intake
- document questions
- deadline questions
- appointment request intake
- case or policy status questions
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- legal, tax, financial, or insurance advice
- official deadlines or filings
- case strategy or coverage decisions
- final appointment confirmation
- fees and contracts
- sensitive documents

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For legal, tax, accounting, and insurance use cases, Ganexity should not provide professional advice or official filing guidance. It should capture the enquiry for human review.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
