# AI Phone Receptionist for Gyms

Business type: Gyms
Region focus: Spain and Valencia
Primary phone problem: Gyms receive calls while staff are helping members or running classes.
Spanish keywords: gimnasio, asistente telefónico IA, recepcionista telefónica

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Fitness and sports teams are often teaching classes, coaching, or managing members. Ganexity can capture membership interest, preferred schedules, and trial-session requests for human follow-up.

## Common incoming calls

- membership questions
- class schedule questions
- trial session requests
- prices and plan questions
- rescheduling
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- health advice
- final membership terms
- class capacity confirmation
- payments and discounts
- cancellations
- sensitive cases

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
