# AI Phone Receptionist for Rental Car Companies

Business type: Rental Car Companies
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Rental car companies receive calls about vehicle availability, dates, documents, and prices.
Spanish keywords: alquiler coches, rent a car, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Transport and logistics teams coordinate routes, drivers, passengers, and deliveries while calls arrive. Ganexity can capture pickup, delivery, route, timing, and contact details for dispatch review.

## Common incoming calls

- pickup or delivery request intake
- route and timing questions
- availability questions
- passenger or load details
- status updates
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- dispatch confirmation
- final price
- vehicle availability
- delivery promise
- insurance decisions
- emergency response

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For transport and logistics use cases, Ganexity should not confirm dispatch, vehicle availability, price, delivery promises, or emergency response. It should capture the request for staff review.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
