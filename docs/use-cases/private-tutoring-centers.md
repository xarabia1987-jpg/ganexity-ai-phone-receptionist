# AI Phone Receptionist for Private Tutoring Centers

Business type: Private Tutoring Centers
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Tutoring centers receive parent enquiries while teachers are in lessons.
Spanish keywords: clases particulares, academia refuerzo, asistente telefónico IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Academies receive repetitive enquiries during classes, enrollment periods, and schedule changes. Ganexity can capture course interest, level, preferred schedule, and callback requests for human follow-up.

## Common incoming calls

- course information and prices
- levels, schedules, and age groups
- enrollment documents
- trial class requests
- rescheduling
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final enrollment acceptance
- official schedules
- placement decisions
- discounts and prices
- payments and refunds
- sensitive student cases

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
