# AI Phone Receptionist for Orthopedic Stores

Business type: Orthopedic Stores
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Orthopedic stores receive calls about mobility products, sizes, repairs, and advice requests.
Spanish keywords: ortopedia, tienda ortopédica, captación de leads telefónicos

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Health retail businesses serve customers in-store while callers ask about products, appointments, prescriptions, and support. Ganexity can capture the request and escalate suitability or medical questions.

## Common incoming calls

- product availability questions
- appointment request intake
- prescription or equipment questions
- delivery or repair questions
- service questions
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- medical advice
- prescription interpretation
- stock confirmation
- final price
- delivery date
- product suitability decisions

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For health retail use cases, Ganexity should not give medical advice, interpret prescriptions, or confirm product suitability. It should capture the request for staff review.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
