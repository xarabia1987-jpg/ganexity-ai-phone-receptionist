# AI Phone Receptionist for Private Schools

Business type: Private Schools
Region focus: Spain and Valencia
Primary phone problem: Private schools receive admissions and parent calls during school hours.
Spanish keywords: colegio privado, captación de llamadas, recepcionista telefónica con IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Care and family services handle trust-sensitive calls while staff are responsible for people. Ganexity can capture the request, urgency, and contact details, then escalate human decisions.

## Common incoming calls

- availability questions
- admission or intake requests
- care need descriptions
- documents and schedule questions
- urgent family concerns
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- admissions or acceptance decisions
- care plans
- safeguarding or medical decisions
- prices and contracts
- final schedule confirmation
- sensitive family cases

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Safety note

For childcare, elder care, and home care use cases, Ganexity should not make safeguarding, medical, admission, or care decisions. It should collect information and escalate.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
