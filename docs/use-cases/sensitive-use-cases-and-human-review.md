# Sensitive Use Cases and Human Review

Ganexity is designed for reliable phone intake, missed-call capture, busy-line support, and structured lead summaries. It should not act as a final decision-maker in sensitive or commitment-heavy situations.

## Human review should remain mandatory for

- final prices, discounts, quotes, and payment commitments
- final appointment, booking, reservation, or installation confirmation
- stock availability, delivery dates, and installation dates
- legal, tax, financial, insurance, medical, or clinical advice
- diagnosis, emergency triage, dispatch, or urgent safety decisions
- complaints, refunds, cancellations, and conflict situations
- contracts, permits, documents, filings, and official deadlines
- sensitive family, childcare, elder care, patient, or pet health cases

## Healthcare and clinics

The AI receptionist may capture the caller's name, phone number, reason for calling, preferred callback time, and urgency. It should not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, emergency triage, or clinical decisions.

## Legal, tax, accounting, and insurance

The AI receptionist may capture the enquiry type, deadline, documents mentioned, and contact information. It should not provide legal advice, tax advice, financial advice, insurance coverage decisions, or official filing guidance.

## Emergency-like local services

For locksmiths, plumbers, HVAC companies, taxis, veterinary emergency services, and similar businesses, the AI receptionist may capture the request and urgency. It should not promise immediate dispatch, arrival times, availability, or emergency outcomes.

## Appointments and reservations

Ganexity can capture appointment requests, reservation requests, preferred times, party size, service interest, and notes. Final confirmation should remain human-approved unless the business has a reliable, explicit, and tested system for confirming availability.

## Safe default

When uncertain, Ganexity should collect the lead, mark human follow-up as required, and send a clear structured summary to the team.
