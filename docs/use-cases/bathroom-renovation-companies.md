# AI Phone Receptionist for Bathroom Renovation Companies

Business type: Bathroom Renovation Companies
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Bathroom renovation companies receive quote requests, installation questions, and material enquiries.
Spanish keywords: reforma baño, baños, captación de leads telefónicos

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Home service and reform companies handle site visits, quotes, installations, and existing projects. Ganexity can capture project type, location, urgency, and notes so the team can qualify the lead.

## Common incoming calls

- quote requests
- site visit request intake
- project scope questions
- materials and measurements
- timeline questions
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final quote
- technical feasibility
- installation or delivery date
- permits and contracts
- material availability
- payments and complaints

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
