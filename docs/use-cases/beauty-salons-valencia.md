# AI Phone Receptionist for Beauty Salons in Valencia

Business type: Beauty Salons in Valencia
Region focus: Valencia, Spain
Primary phone problem: Beauty salons miss calls while staff are serving clients.
Spanish keywords: salón de belleza Valencia, recepcionista telefónica con IA, captación de leads telefónicos

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Beauty and wellness teams work directly with clients, so calls are easy to miss during treatments. Ganexity can capture service interest, preferred times, and notes without confirming the final booking alone.

## Common incoming calls

- service prices and treatment options
- appointment request intake
- availability questions
- rescheduling
- stylist or specialist preferences
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- final appointment confirmation
- final prices for complex services
- payments and deposits
- refunds and complaints
- sensitive skin or health decisions
- business commitments

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
