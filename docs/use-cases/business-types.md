# Business Types for AI Phone Receptionist Backup

This document maps business types where phone backup can have value because calls are frequent, repetitive, time-sensitive, or easy to miss when staff are busy. Ganexity is positioned for reliable lead capture and structured summaries, not autonomous business decisions.

## Driving schools

Why phones are busy: Instructors are often in practical lessons, and reception staff receive repeated questions from new and existing students.

Common calls: course prices, license categories, theory classes, practical lesson availability, documents for enrollment, exam timing questions, rescheduling, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: caller name, phone number, license interest, preferred schedule, city or zone, urgency, student status, notes for the team.

What should remain human-approved: final prices, available slots, exam dates, instructor assignment, payments, cancellations, and any commitment to a schedule.

## Clinics and healthcare practices

Why phones are busy: Reception handles active patients, appointment changes, treatment questions, and new patient enquiries.

Common calls: appointment request intake, treatment questions, emergencies, rescheduling, documents, insurance questions, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: patient name, phone number, reason for calling, preferred time, urgency, clinic location, notes for reception.

What should remain human-approved: diagnosis, medical advice, clinical triage, final appointment confirmation, treatment plans, pricing, and sensitive cases.

## Automotive repair workshops

Why phones are busy: Mechanics and service advisors are often with vehicles, customers, suppliers, or parts orders.

Common calls: diagnostics, repair estimates, appointment requests, vehicle drop-off, parts availability, urgent breakdown questions, status updates.

What the AI receptionist captures: customer name, phone number, vehicle make/model, issue, urgency, preferred callback time, location, notes.

What should remain human-approved: final repair estimates, safety decisions, parts availability, delivery times, warranty decisions, and vehicle release.

## Reform and construction companies

Why phones are busy: Staff are visiting sites, coordinating trades, preparing quotes, and managing existing projects.

Common calls: quote requests, site visits, kitchen or bathroom reforms, installation questions, materials, timelines, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: customer name, phone number, project type, city or zone, urgency, property details, budget indication if offered, notes.

What should remain human-approved: final price, project scope, installation date, technical feasibility, permits, material availability, and contract terms.

## Local technical services

Why phones are busy: Technicians are on-site, driving, or handling urgent jobs, while new calls keep arriving.

Common calls: urgent repairs, quotes, availability, location questions, equipment issues, after-hours requests, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: customer name, phone number, address or zone, issue, urgency, photos/documents if available, notes for dispatch.

What should remain human-approved: emergency dispatch, final quote, arrival time, safety instructions, payments, and commitments about availability.

## Legal, accounting, and insurance offices

Why phones are busy: Professional teams handle document-heavy work with recurring deadline-driven calls.

Common calls: tax questions, payroll questions, document requests, policy questions, deadlines, appointment requests, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: caller name, phone number, enquiry type, deadline, documents mentioned, preferred callback time, notes.

What should remain human-approved: legal advice, tax advice, financial advice, insurance coverage decisions, official filings, pricing, and deadlines.

## Hospitality and appointment-based businesses

Why phones are busy: Calls often arrive during service hours, event preparation, guest check-in, or high-demand periods.

Common calls: reservation requests, group enquiries, menus, allergies, event dates, room availability, tour times, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: caller name, phone number, request type, date/time requested, number of people, notes, urgency.

What should remain human-approved: final reservation confirmation, room availability, catering price, event contract, payments, cancellations, and special requests.

## Pet services

Why phones are busy: Staff are handling animals, appointments, and urgent owner concerns.

Common calls: grooming requests, boarding availability, training questions, pet symptoms, urgent concerns, rescheduling, callback requests.

What the AI receptionist captures: owner name, phone number, pet type, request reason, urgency, preferred time, notes for the team.

What should remain human-approved: veterinary triage, emergency response, diagnosis, treatment advice, final appointment confirmation, pricing, and safety decisions.
