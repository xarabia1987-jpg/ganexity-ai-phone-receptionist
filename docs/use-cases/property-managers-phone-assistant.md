# AI Phone Receptionist for Property Managers

Business type: Property Managers
Region focus: Spain
Primary phone problem: Property managers receive tenant, owner, maintenance, and viewing calls.
Spanish keywords: administrador de fincas, property manager, asistente telefónico IA

## Why this business type needs phone backup

Property teams receive high lead volume about listings, viewings, rentals, sales, and maintenance. Ganexity can capture the caller profile, zone, property interest, and urgency for follow-up.

## Common incoming calls

- property availability questions
- viewing request intake
- buyer or renter profile questions
- zone and budget questions
- maintenance or tenant issues
- callback requests

## What the AI receptionist captures

- customer name
- phone number
- reason for calling
- preferred time for callback or appointment request
- city, zone, address, or service area when relevant
- urgency
- notes for the team
- missing information that staff may need to ask later

## What should remain human-approved

- property availability confirmation
- final viewing confirmation
- offers, deposits, and contracts
- legal documents
- maintenance commitments
- business commitments

## Best use case

Backup when the line is busy, after-hours, during lunch breaks, and during peak demand. Ganexity should capture the request and create a clear follow-up task instead of pretending to close the case alone.

## Output

Clear structured call summary sent to the team through Google Sheets, CRM, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com, webhook, or another reliable workflow.
