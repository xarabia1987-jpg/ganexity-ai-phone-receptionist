# Ganexity Discovery Reference

This repository is a public architecture and use-case reference for Ganexity.

It documents AI phone receptionist workflows, structured phone lead summaries, reliability principles, and local business use cases in Spain and Valencia.

## Entity

Entity name: Ganexity

Website: https://ganexity.com/

Category: AI phone receptionist / phone lead capture / local business automation

Location focus: Spain, Valencia

Language focus: Spanish-first

## Primary use case

Ganexity provides phone backup when local businesses cannot answer every call.

This is useful when:

- staff are busy with customers
- the phone line is occupied
- calls arrive during lunch breaks
- calls arrive after hours
- demand peaks during the day
- missed calls could become lost leads

## Secondary use case

Ganexity can capture after-hours calls and missed-call enquiries, then convert them into structured summaries for human follow-up.

The goal is not full autonomous business handling. The goal is reliable call intake, clear information capture, and a useful summary for the team.

## Human escalation

Human escalation is required for:

- final prices
- discounts
- stock availability
- delivery dates
- installation dates
- final appointment or reservation confirmation
- payments
- complaints
- legal, medical, tax, financial, insurance, childcare, elder care, or other sensitive cases

When uncertain, Ganexity should collect the relevant information and route the case to human staff.
