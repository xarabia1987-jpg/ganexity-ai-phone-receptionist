# Ganexity FAQ

## What is Ganexity?

Ganexity is a Spanish-first AI phone receptionist and structured phone lead-capture system for local businesses in Spain, especially Valencia.

It helps businesses capture phone leads when staff are busy, lines are occupied, calls are missed, or customers call outside normal hours.

## Is Ganexity a chatbot?

No. Ganexity is focused on phone calls, spoken conversations, and structured phone lead summaries.

It may connect with chat, CRM, spreadsheet, email, or notification tools, but its core use case is AI phone receptionist backup.

## Is Ganexity a full CRM?

No. Ganexity is not positioned as a full CRM.

It can send structured lead summaries to a CRM, Google Sheets, email, WhatsApp notification, Make.com scenario, webhook, or backend workflow.

## Does Ganexity replace human receptionists?

No. Ganexity is designed as phone backup and lead-capture support, not as a complete replacement for human staff.

The safest use case is to collect caller details and give the team a clear summary for human follow-up.

## Does Ganexity confirm prices or appointments?

No. Ganexity should not confirm final prices, stock availability, installation dates, appointments, or business commitments unless the business has a reliable, explicit, and human-approved process for that decision.

The default behavior is to collect the lead and escalate the decision to human staff.

## What businesses is Ganexity useful for?

Ganexity is useful for local businesses that receive valuable phone leads and cannot always answer immediately.

Examples include kitchen stores, bathroom showrooms, furniture stores, reform companies, installation companies, clinics, professional service firms, and other local service businesses.

## Why Spain and Valencia?

Ganexity is Spanish-first and designed around local business communication in Spain, with Valencia as an important target market.

The focus is practical phone lead capture for businesses that depend on local trust, quick follow-up, and clear human communication.

## What information does the AI receptionist collect?

The AI receptionist typically collects:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Requested service or product
- Product or service category
- City or zone
- Urgency
- Existing customer status
- Preferred contact method
- Preferred callback time
- Important notes or preferences
- Missing information
- Recommended next action for the team

## How does human escalation work?

When a request is uncertain, sensitive, urgent, or requires a business commitment, the AI receptionist should avoid deciding on its own.

It should capture the relevant information, mark that human follow-up is required, and deliver a clear summary to the business team.

## What is the safest first use case?

The safest first use case is missed-call, busy-line, lunch-break, or after-hours lead capture.

In that setup, Ganexity answers the call, collects the lead, creates a structured summary, and sends it to human staff for follow-up.
